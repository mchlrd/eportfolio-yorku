## Purpose

This portfolio exists to make growth visible. A résumé lists what you did; this site is meant to show the harder thing — how each piece of work changed the way I think about engineering, and where my judgement is still weak.

Everything here is organised around one direction: to become an engineer who can both build systems and defend them. Each artifact is tagged with at least one Lassonde undergraduate competency and paired with an honest reflection, so a reader can follow the reasoning and not just the result.

## How to read this site

Each artifact carries two things the course asks for: a tag naming the Lassonde competency it demonstrates, and a reflection written to answer three questions — what did I create or do, what did I learn, and how does this connect to my goals. The competency is never just a tag; every reflection argues for it.

A note on the interface: this is a hand-built static site rather than a themed site builder, so the layout imitates the tools I actually work in — a file tree, a line-number gutter, a status bar. Everything on it is readable without JavaScript, and it prints cleanly.

## Competencies

The twelve Lassonde undergraduate competencies, which act as the reading lens for every artifact in this portfolio.

  • 01Knowledge Base for Engineering — fundamentals applied with intent.
  • 02Problem Analysis — framing ambiguous problems into solvable ones.
  • 03Investigation — evidence gathering, experimentation, validation.
  • 04Design — building solutions to a defined need under constraints.
  • 05Use of Engineering Tools — choosing and operating the right instruments.
  • 06Individual and Teamwork — working alone well, and with others better.
  • 07Communication Skills — writing and speaking for a real audience.
  • 08Professionalism — reliability, judgement, accountability.
  • 09Impact on Society and the Environment — consequences beyond the system.
  • 10Ethics and Equity — doing the right thing when it is inconvenient.
  • 11Economics and Project Management — cost, trade-offs, delivery.
  • 12Life-Long Learning — deliberate, self-directed growth.
