Purpose

Why this portfolio exists.

A résumé lists what you did. This portfolio is meant to show something harder to fake: how each piece of work changed the way I think. It is a living document — I add to it after every term, every placement, and every project that teaches me something I didn't expect.

Everything here is organised around a single direction: to become an engineer who can both build systems and defend them. Each artifact is tagged with at least one Lassonde undergraduate competency and paired with an honest reflection, so a reader can follow the judgement behind the work, not just the outcome.

How to read this site

Start with About Me for context, move to Career for the record, then open Course Projects for the evidence. Every artifact carries a Retell → Relate → Reflect reflection and an explicit competency justification.
