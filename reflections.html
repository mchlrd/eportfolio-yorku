Section D · Reflections

More than what happened.

A reflection is not a summary. It is an honest, analytical response to an experience — and every artifact in this portfolio has one attached, indexed here so nothing is buried.

The framework

Retell. Relate. Reflect.

Step 1

Retell

What happened? Briefly describe the experience, project, or task. Set the scene without padding it out — description is the floor, not the ceiling.

Step 2

Relate

How does this connect to what I'm learning in COOP 2100? Which concepts, frameworks, or competencies apply — and why those, specifically?

Step 3

Reflect

What did I learn about myself? What would I do differently? How has this changed my thinking? This is where the growth is demonstrated.

The standard I'm holding myself to

Reflections that only Retell don't demonstrate anything — they describe activity and leave the reader to guess at the learning. Every reflection on this site is written so that the Relate and Reflect sections carry the argument, and each one names the competency it demonstrates and explains the connection rather than simply tagging it.
